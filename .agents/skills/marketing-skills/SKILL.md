# Marketing Skills

A unified framework for growth engineering, conversion optimization, high-converting copywriting, and product positioning.

## Core Pillars & Skills

1. **Foundations & Positioning**:
   - `product-marketing`: Captures product positioning, ideal customer profile (ICP), and core value propositions in `.agents/product-marketing.md`.
   - `marketing-psychology`: Loss aversion, social proof, urgency, anchoring, and cognitive friction reduction.
   - `customer-research`: Voice-of-customer synthesis and objection handling.

2. **Conversion & Copywriting**:
   - `copywriting`: PAS (Problem-Agitate-Solution), AIDA, headline formulas, and benefit-driven value statements.
   - `copy-editing`: Tightening prose, eliminating passive voice, and boosting emotional resonance.
   - `cro`: Landing page optimization, call-to-action (CTA) hierarchy, above-the-fold clarity, and friction elimination.
   - `onboarding`: First-time user experience (FTUE), time-to-value (TTV) acceleration, and empty states.

3. **Retention & Growth**:
   - `cold-email` & `emails`: Engaging lifecycle emails, follow-up cadences, and subject line testing.
   - `marketing-loops`: Viral coefficient, referral loops, and user-generated flywheel mechanics.
   - `seo-audit` & `ai-seo`: Schema markup, search intent matching, and LLM citation readiness.

## Golden Rules
- **Benefit over Feature**: Never lead with mechanics; lead with candidate transformation.
- **Concrete over Vague**: Use real numbers, verifiable proofs, and clear timelines.
- **Single Dominant Action**: Every screen has one unambiguous primary next step.
- **Reduce Cognitive Friction**: Remove extraneous options, clarify inputs, and provide immediate reassurance.
